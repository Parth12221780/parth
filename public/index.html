Satta King — How This Result Page Works

Everything you need to understand the live board, timings and historical record charts.

01

What the result table shows

The table records each market's declared result for the day. Once a number is entered by the administrator, it appears here and remains part of the historical archive.

02

Reading the live board and timings

The live board shows the latest available status for each market. Until a result is confirmed and entered, the board displays Wait rather than a placeholder number.

03

Using the record charts

The monthly grid places all active markets side by side. Use Previous and Next to browse months, or open a market's Record Chart for its year-wise archive.

04

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is the Satta King result declared?

Each market carries its own fixed time, printed beside its name in the result table. The number appears here after confirmation, so checking around that time is enough.

How far back do the record charts go?

Day-wise records are archived from 2015 through 2026. Open any market in the record chart list to browse a complete year in one table.

Do you sell numbers or accept money?

No. We publish recorded results only. We never sell predictions, take stakes, or ask a visitor to pay for anything.

Why does a market show “Wait”?

Because the operator has not declared that draw yet. The figure replaces “Wait” automatically as soon as it is recorded.
